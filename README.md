## App Features (v2)

- **Authentication & Roles**: Drivers and Passengers with normalized phone formatting (+265 / 0 stripping).
- **Driver Verification**: Vehicle registration & model submission with image picker and simulated admin approval.
- **Ride Creation**: Drivers can publish routes, dates, times, available seats, and seat pricing in MWK.
- **Search & Booking**: Passengers can search routes, view driver ratings and vehicle details, and request seats.
- **Request Management**: Drivers can accept or decline seat requests; seat availability dynamically decrements on acceptance.
- **Contact Shortcuts**: Instant Call and SMS shortcuts for confirmed bookings.
